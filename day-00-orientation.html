01 What this certification is

A design exam, not a hands-on lab.

  • Validates that you can design solutions on AWS against the Well-Architected Framework.
  • You are given a scenario and pick the best architecture. The whole exam is multiple choice, so you never touch a console or write a line of code.
  • AWS assumes ~1 year hands-on designing on AWS, but that is a recommendation rather than a gate; nothing stops you booking today.
  • There is no prerequisite certification. Cloud Practitioner is optional.
Note Exam code matters

SAA-C03 is the current and only live version. Ignore blogs advertising an "SAA-C04": AWS publishes no such exam guide, and that URL returns 404. Verified against AWS docs, September 2026.

03 How scoring actually works

Three things people get wrong.

Good news Compensatory scoring

You do not need to pass each domain individually, only the exam overall. A weak domain can be carried by a strong one. The per-domain feedback on your score report is directional only; AWS explicitly warns against over-reading it.

Good news Guessing is free

There is no penalty for a wrong answer, and unanswered questions are scored as wrong anyway. A guess is strictly better than a blank. Never leave anything empty — flag it and move on, but put an answer down first.

05 Two ways to sit the exam

The exam and the fee are identical either way; what differs is how each one can go wrong. For most candidates in Nepal, online proctored is the realistic default, but only if your power and connection hold for 130 minutes uninterrupted.

In person Pearson VUE test centre

  • ID: two primary IDs, or one primary + one secondary
  • Arrive: 15–30 min early
  • Belongings: stored by staff before you enter the room
  • Breaks: unscheduled breaks allowed, the timer keeps running; you cannot leave the building
  • Kit: their machine, their network

At home OnVUE online proctored

  • ID: one primary, government-issued, photo + signature in Roman characters
  • Check-in: opens 30 min early; more than 15 min late forfeits the fee
  • Room: 360° webcam scan, private space, nobody may enter
  • Breaks: none; leaving camera view ends the exam
  • Kit: your machine, your internet, your risk
Where they differ

Caution Load-shedding and bandwidth

OnVUE holds a live webcam and screen-share for 130 minutes or more. If your power or link is not dependable for that whole window uninterrupted, take the test centre. A drop mid-exam is your loss, not theirs.

06 ESL +30, the free extra 30 minutes

English is not your first language, so you are entitled to 30 extra minutes at no cost. Claim them.

  1. Create or sign in to your AWS Certification Account.
  2. Go to Request Exam Accommodations → Request Accommodation.
  3. Accommodation type → ESL +30 MINUTES → Create.
  4. Approved automatically, with no documentation or proof of nationality required.
  5. Now schedule the exam. The extension is applied at booking.
Danger Order of operations

You must request this before you schedule. If an exam is already booked you cannot add it; you have to cancel the booking, request the accommodation, then rebook, and cancelling inside 24 hours forfeits the fee. Do this on day one, before you even think about a date.

Good news One-time

It saves to your account permanently and auto-applies to every future AWS exam, with every delivery provider. Request once, benefit forever.

07 Booking from Kathmandu

Always book through AWS, never directly on Pearson VUE.

  1. Create an AWS Certification Account (AWS Builder ID).
  2. Fix your name first. The account name must match your passport or citizenship ID exactly. See section 09.
  3. Request ESL +30, see section 06. Before scheduling, not after.
  4. Schedule New Exam → find SAA-C03 → Schedule with Pearson VUE.
  5. Choose test centre (search Kathmandu) or online proctored, then date and slot.
  6. Pay in USD. Keep the confirmation email; it carries the reschedule link.
Caution Finding a centre: do this yourself, live

Do not trust any centre list found by searching, this note included. Third-party pages are stale and often wrong: one widely-cited "Kathmandu Pearson VUE centre" page resolves to an address in Kolkata, and another centre's site is dead.

Two things actually matter. First, Pearson VUE runs separate programs: many Nepali centres are PTE Academic (English testing) only, while AWS needs a Pearson Professional centre. A centre being "Pearson VUE authorised" does not mean it delivers AWS.

Second, the only authoritative list is the one shown inside the AWS scheduling flow after you pick Pearson VUE. It is filtered to centres that actually deliver AWS exams. Search "Kathmandu" there and take whatever it returns.

Note If nothing appears in Kathmandu

Fall back to OnVUE online proctored: same exam, same fee, no travel.

09 Traps that fail people before question 1

Every one of these is admin, not knowledge, which means every one is avoidable in a single afternoon. That is what Do this week is for.

Failure modes and prevention
TrapLevelConsequencePrevention
Name mismatch Danger Turned away at the door. Fee gone. Account name must equal ID name character for character. Fix weeks ahead; it needs AWS support, not a settings toggle.
ESL requested too late Danger Cannot add it; must cancel and rebook, forfeiting the fee if inside 24 hours. Request before scheduling. Day one.
Expired ID Danger Entry refused. Fee gone. Check the expiry date now, not the night before.
One ID at a test centre Danger Entry refused. Fee gone. Centres want two. Carry passport plus licence or citizenship.
Late for OnVUE Danger More than 15 min late forfeits the fee. Start check-in at the 30-minute mark, not at start time.
Handwritten notes on OnVUE Danger Exam terminated, score invalidated. Clear the desk completely. Use the built-in whiteboard.
Blank answers Caution Scored as wrong. Guess on everything. There is no downside.
