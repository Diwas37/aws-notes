AWS Certified Solutions Architect — Associate

What the exam is, how it is scored, how to sit it from Kathmandu, and the rules that disqualify people before they answer a single question.

SAA-C03 Associate level 130 min 65 questions USD 150 Pass 720/1000

01What this certification is

A design exam, not a hands-on lab.

  • Validates that you can design solutions on AWS against the Well-Architected Framework.
  • You are given a scenario and pick the best architecture. No console work, no CLI, no coding.
  • AWS assumes ~1 year hands-on designing on AWS. That is a recommendation, not a gate. Nothing blocks you from booking today.
  • No prerequisite certification. Cloud Practitioner is optional, not required.
Exam code matters

SAA-C03 is the current and only live version. Ignore blogs advertising an "SAA-C04" — AWS publishes no such exam guide, and that URL returns 404. Verified against AWS docs, September 2026.

03How scoring actually works

Three things people get wrong.

Compensatory scoring

You do not need to pass each domain. Only the overall score matters. A weak domain can be carried by a strong one. The per-domain feedback on your score report is directional only — AWS explicitly warns against over-reading it.

No penalty for guessing

Unanswered questions are scored as wrong. A guess is strictly better than a blank. Never leave anything empty. Flag and move on, but always put an answer down first.

05Two ways to sit the exam

Same exam, same price, very different failure modes.

Mode A

Pearson VUE test center

  • ID: two primary IDs, or one primary + one secondary
  • Arrive: 15–30 min early
  • Belongings: stored by staff before you enter the room
  • Breaks: unscheduled breaks allowed, timer keeps running; cannot leave the building
  • Kit: their machine, their network

Mode B

OnVUE online proctored

  • ID: one primary, government-issued, photo + signature in Roman characters
  • Check-in: opens 30 min early; >15 min late = fee forfeited
  • Room: 360° webcam scan, private space, nobody may enter
  • Breaks: none — leaving camera view ends the exam
  • Kit: your machine, your internet, your risk

Load-shedding & bandwidth

OnVUE holds a live webcam + screen-share for 130+ minutes. If your power or link is not dependable for that full window uninterrupted, take the test center. A drop mid-exam is your loss, not theirs.

06ESL +30 — the free extra 30 minutes

English is not your first language. Claim this. It costs nothing.

  1. Create / sign in to your AWS Certification Account.
  2. Go to Request Exam Accommodations → Request Accommodation.
  3. Accommodation type → ESL +30 MINUTES → Create.
  4. Approved automatically. No documentation, no proof of nationality, no waiting.
  5. Now schedule the exam. The extension is applied at booking.
Order of operations — this is the trap

You must request it before you schedule. If an exam is already booked you cannot add it — you have to cancel the booking, request the accommodation, then rebook. Do this on day one, before you even think about a date.

One-time

It saves to your account permanently and auto-applies to every future AWS exam, with every delivery provider. Request once, benefit forever.

07Booking from Kathmandu

Always book through AWS, never directly on Pearson VUE.

  1. Create an AWS Certification Account (AWS Builder ID).
  2. Fix your name first. Account name must match your passport / citizenship ID exactly. See the trap below.
  3. Request ESL +30 (section 06) — before scheduling.
  4. Schedule New Exam → find SAA-C03 → Schedule with Pearson VUE.
  5. Choose test center (search Kathmandu) or online proctored, then date and slot.
  6. Pay in USD. Keep the confirmation email — it carries the reschedule link.
Finding a Kathmandu center — do this yourself, live

Do not trust any center list found by searching, this note included. Third-party pages are stale and often wrong: one widely-cited "Kathmandu Pearson VUE center" page resolves to an address in Kolkata, and another center's site is dead.

Two things that actually matter:

① Pearson VUE runs separate programs. Many Nepali centers are PTE Academic (English testing) only. AWS needs a Pearson Professional / IT certification center. A center being "Pearson VUE authorised" does not mean it delivers AWS.

② The only authoritative list is the one shown inside the AWS scheduling flow after you pick Pearson VUE — it is filtered to centers that actually deliver AWS exams. Search "Kathmandu" there and take whatever it returns.

If nothing appears in Kathmandu

Fall back to OnVUE online proctored — same exam, same fee, no travel. That is the realistic default for most candidates in Nepal.
